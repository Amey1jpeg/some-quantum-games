Wave Gauntlet: drag the white dot to move the corridor, the coloured dots to resize the gap, click empty space to add a keyframe. Spider: click to add a block (top half = ceiling, bottom half = floor), click a block to remove it, drag to move. Right-click deletes. Ctrl/Cmd+Z undoes; deleted drafts go to the trash.
Deco mode places harmless scenery that renders behind the corridor — pick a shape and colour, click to place, drag to move, right-click to delete. Stamp mode drops whole pre-made sections (slam, sawtooth, needle…) and shifts the rest of the level right to fit.
Portal mode takes two clicks: the first is where you go in (blue), the second is where you come out (orange). They can be on the same column or far apart, at any height. Crossing the entry column takes you through — there is nothing to aim for. Drag the blue end to move it, right-click to delete. Works in Wave Gauntlet and UFO, and both the checker and the bot follow them.
✓ Check catches gaps the wave physically can't reach and flips there isn't time to make. 📤 Submit checks your level, then copies it — send that to Arham and he can add it to the game. Nothing here uploads anywhere; everything saves in your own browser.

Entangled Ascent: pick a tool and click to place it. The top half is your gap, the bottom half the twin's. With Select, drag a column's white gap edges to resize, drag inside a gap to move it, drag the spikes to move the column; drag blocks, portals, orbs and the finish line anywhere. Drag empty space or scroll to pan. Spikes: column, thick column, half wall (one half only), spike block (set a slide speed to make it move), saw, and spike strip (snaps to the ceiling, either side of the mirror line, or the floor, by where you click). 🏁 Finish ends the level the moment the dots reach its x, at any height (the first one wins; otherwise the level ends at the finish line). Desync start/end triggers mark a section where the link is forced broken — W/S moves you, ↑/↓ your twin. Right-click or Delete removes. Columns turn red when the gaps don't mirror (DESYNC). ✓ Check flags columns the dots can't reach in time (assuming any fast portal before them was taken). ▶ Test Play opens the level in the game. Set each level's Difficulty (Easy → Frame Perfect) in Properties; the game plays built-in levels easiest first. Making a level playable for everyone: under Built-in, press + Add current draft (or + Paste level JSON), then ⬇ Save built-in file and replace js/ascent-builtin.js in the repo with the downloaded file and commit it.
